### 1. The Observation
In 2017, the interstellar object 'Oumuamua (1I/2017 U1) passed through our solar system. Unlike a standard comet or asteroid, it exhibited a significant **non-gravitational acceleration**. It sped up as it left the sun, but showed no signs of "outgassing" (no cometary tail or dust clouds) that could explain the thrust.

### 2. The Standard Model Failure
Mainstream astrophysics struggled to identify the mechanism behind this extra velocity. 
* **The Comet Hypothesis:** Failed because no water or gas emission was detected. 
* **The Hydrogen Iceberg Theory:** Suggested solid hydrogen sublimated into invisible gas, but this was criticized for being physically unlikely over interstellar distances.
* **The "Light Sail" Suggestion:** Proposed it was a thin artificial sail, which remains speculative.

Under standard gravitational physics, 'Oumuamua should have followed a predictable ballistic trajectory. It did not.
