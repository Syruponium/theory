## The Equation of State: c, 10^34 Pa, and 13.84 K

This repository contains the foundational data and models for the SMP theory, which proposes that 'Dark Matter' is a temperature-dependent phase transition of space itself.

### The Core Hypothesis
Space-time is a high-pressure superfluid (The Matrix) that 'snaps' into a viscous state at a critical thermal threshold of **13.84 K**.

### Fundamental Relationship
The radius where galactic rotation curves become flat (R_snap) is proportional to the square root of the Bolometric Luminosity (L_bol):

$$R_{snap} \propto \sqrt{L_{bol}}$$
