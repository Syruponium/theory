**The principle:** Universal expansion is an active mechanical reaction to matter creation, acting as a perpetual, self-sustaining feedback loop rather than relying on Dark Energy.

`The equation:` $S_m = \frac{V_e}{M_c} \cdot k$

LAW_002_ELASTIC_FEEDBACK

**Description:**
A Matrix Rip lowers local tension $(S_m)$) by converting potential energy into mass ($M_c$). The resulting heat and kinetic energy increase internal pressure, forcing the Matrix to expand ($V_e)$. 

This physical stretch pushes distant regions outward, causing tension ($S_m$) in those areas to climb back toward the $10^{34}$ Pa limit, restarting the cycle.

**Constraint:**
Matrix Tension ($S_m$) is constantly driven towards, but capped by, the $10^{34}$ Pa threshold.

**Invariant:**
Perpetual mechanical imbalance drives cosmic expansion continuously.

**Scale Invariance & Fractal Consistency**

- **Macro (Cosmic):** Universal expansion as a mechanical elastic reaction to localized matter injection.
- **Micro (Terrestrial):** Analogous to the elastic deformation of a polymer solution; injecting matter or shifting volume locally forces the entire network to stretch to redistribute internal tension.
