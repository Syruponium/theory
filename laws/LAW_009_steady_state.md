LAW_009_STEADY_STATE

**Description:**
Matter moving through the rigid Matrix generates heat ($Q$) via Matrix Drag. To prevent this heat from breaching the $10^{34}$ Pa limit ($P_{limit}$), the universe safely dissipates it via adiabatic expansion ($dV/dt$). To offset the density drop caused by this newly created space, black holes locally "print" new matter (Helium-3) to maintain equilibrium.

**Constraint:**
Expansion Efficiency ($dV/dm = 2.62 \times 10^{-39} \text{ m}^3/(\text{kg}\cdot\text{s}))$ and Mass Creation Rate ($dm/dt = 2.62 \times 10^{-66} \text{ kg/(m}^3\cdot\text{s})$) must remain exactly calibrated.

**Invariant:**
The overall energy density of the Matrix never thins out; every Joule of friction heat is accounted for.

**Scale Invariance & Fractal Consistency**

- **Macro (Cosmic):** The balance between adiabatic expansion (cooling) and matter injection (heating).
- **Micro (Terrestrial):** A self-regulating hydraulic system with a bypass valve; as internal pressure (heat) rises, volume expands to protect the system's structural integrity.
