### 1.1 Context: The Nature of Supercritical Fluids (SCF)
To understand the Great Phase Shift, one must first understand the state of matter known as a Supercritical Fluid. In standard thermodynamics, a substance exists as either a gas or a liquid, separated by a clear boundary (the meniscus). However, when a medium is subjected to temperatures and pressures above its Critical Point, these distinct phases disappear.

### What is a Supercritical Fluid?
A supercritical fluid is a "hybrid" state of matter. It possesses the unique ability to:
* Effuse through solids like a gas: It has no surface tension and nearly zero viscosity, allowing it to penetrate any structure.
* Dissolve materials like a liquid: It has a high density, making it a powerful universal solvent.
